WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT

Your Class Rep is all about reading your opponents, making clever predictions, and pulling off surprising plays. Every student has a unique ability, so every round creates new opportunities to bluff, adapt, and outsmart the competition. Games are quick enough for "just one more round," yet packed with meaningful decisions from start to finish.

HOW IT FEELS

Choose your student in secret. Reveal together. Watch abilities collide in unexpected ways. Celebrate your victories, laugh at the surprises, and race to earn the most votes before the election is over.
