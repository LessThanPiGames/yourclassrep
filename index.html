Every card brings three things to the election: Influence, an Ability, and Votes.

Influence determines who comes out on top each round. Abilities give you ways to change what happens. Votes are what ultimately decide who becomes Class Rep.

The trick is knowing which card to play, and when.

HOW TO PLAY

01

CHOOSE

Both players secretly choose one card.

02

REVEAL

Reveal your cards at the same time.

03

RESOLVE

Apply Influential Abilities and compare Influence. If abilities conflict, the higher Influence takes precedence.

04

WIN

Winning and tied cards remain face up. Losing cards are placed face down.

05

REPEAT

Repeat steps 1–4 six times. You'll have one card remaining in your hand.

06

BECOME CLASS REP

Resolve the Votes and Voter Abilities of the face-up cards. The player with the most Votes wins the election.
