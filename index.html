YOUR CLASS REP

A fast-paced classroom election card game where every student has a unique ability.

👥 2 Players
⏱ 15 Minutes
🎂 Ages 8+

WHY YOU’LL LIKE IT

Bluff. Strategize. Outvote your opponent using a team of uniquely powerful students. Every round is fast, tense, and different.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Choose a card
2. Reveal simultaneously
3. Use abilities
4. Earn Votes to win
